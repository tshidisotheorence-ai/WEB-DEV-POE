## Vision
To transform home renovation and improvement into a seamless, inspiring, and stress-free journey—empowering homeowners to turn their living spaces into personalized sanctuaries while connecting them with trusted experts.

## Mission
To deliver high-quality craftsmanship, transparent pricing, and smart digital design tools that streamline the home renovation process from initial concept to final execution, ensuring safety, sustainability, and exceptional customer satisfaction.

## Key Features & Functionality
 For Homeowners (Client Portal / App)

Interactive Project Dashboard: Track project status, schedule, milestones, and real-time updates in one central hub.

3D Visualizer & Design Tools: Preview design concepts, room layouts, finishes, and color palettes before construction begins.

Cost Estimator & Transparent Budgeting: Real-time cost calculators with itemized breakdowns and clear milestone-based payment schedules.

Contractor & Designer Matching: Algorithmic or curated matching with verified, licensed, and insured professionals.

Project & Supply Chain Management: Materials ordering, inventory tracking, and trade partner scheduling.

Quality Assurance & Compliance Checklists: On-site inspection logs, safety compliance tracking, and warranty record management.

## Timeline & Milestones

The timeline and milestones of a home improvement project follow a structured, sequential lifecycle designed to ensure seamless execution from initial concept to final delivery. The journey begins with the Discovery Phase (Weeks 1–2), where the team conducts initial consultations, takes site measurements, and establishes budget parameters to align with the client’s vision. Moving into Design & Planning (Weeks 3–5), the project reaches its first major milestone—Design Approval—as 3D renderings, material selections, and detailed cost estimates are finalized and signed off. Once approved, the Permitting & Procurement Phase (Weeks 6–8) prepares the project for pre-construction by securing necessary municipal permits, ordering long-lead materials, and scheduling trade contractors. 

Physical construction kicks off during Demolition & Rough-In (Weeks 9–11), focusing on structural framing, electrical, plumbing, and HVAC installations, concluding with required trade inspections. The project then transitions to Finish Work (Weeks 12–15) to achieve substantial completion, encompassing drywall, painting, flooring, cabinetry, and fixture installations. Finally, the Handover Phase (Week 16) wraps up the project with a final walkthrough, punch list completion, site deep cleaning, and the delivery of warranty packages, successfully returning a fully transformed living space to the homeowner.
